The problem
Ireland is under pressure to reduce emissions, but deep retrofit is too expensive for many homes, especially hard-to-treat housing stock. At the same time, wind energy is being curtailed and wasted.
Deep retrofit cost per home€49,600
Hard-to-treat homes~445,000
Potential EU penalty exposure€8B+
The solution
Use green hydrogen as a drop-in heating pathway. Instead of rebuilding the whole house, replace the burner and use hydrogen produced from surplus or curtailed renewable electricity.
Installation time1 day
Structural changesMinimal
Boiler efficiency98%
Strategic logic
Curtailment → Value Wind → H₂ → Heat Pilot → Scale
The project turns wasted electricity into stored value. It links energy, housing, agriculture, and regional development into one infrastructure logic.
