Current trajectory: ~29%. The gap lives almost entirely in residential heating — 500,000 homes need to be decarbonised by 2030. We've completed 55,000. The conventional prescription — deep retrofit at €49,600 per home — is structurally impossible for roughly 30% of Irish housing stock. Pre-1940s solid-wall stone construction has no cavity to fill. Heat pumps require low-temperature radiators these homes don't have. Only 12% of Irish households say they would try hydrogen heating, despite 53% wanting to change their heating system. The market exists — the demonstration is missing.
