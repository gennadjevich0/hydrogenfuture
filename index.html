The Problem

Ireland must cut GHG emissions 51% by 2030. 500,000 homes need decarbonisation. Deep retrofit costs €49,600/home — impossible for 30% of solid-wall homes. H₂ drop-in boiler: €3–6k, 1-day install, zero structural changes.
