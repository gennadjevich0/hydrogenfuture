The problem
Ireland faces a structural mismatch: climate pressure is rising, deep retrofit is expensive, and a large portion of housing stock remains difficult to decarbonise quickly. At the same time, renewable power is sometimes curtailed when the system cannot absorb it.
Deep retrofit cost per home€49,600
Hard-to-treat segmentVery large
Policy pressure window2030
The solution
Deploy a hydrogen pathway where curtailed or surplus electricity becomes green hydrogen, then use that hydrogen in targeted heating applications where conventional retrofit pathways are too slow, too invasive, or too expensive.
Installation logicDrop-in burner path
Retrofit burdenReduced

System logic
This is not only a heating idea. It is a systems concept that links electricity balancing, hydrogen production, rural transition, infrastructure reuse, and future export potential.
Curtailed wind → Electrolysis → Green hydrogen → Home heat / storage → Regional growth
Pilot roadmap

Phase 1 — Local pilot

Validate the concept in a focused geography with a small number of homes and a clear narrative.

Phase 2 — Production integration

Connect hydrogen production economics to surplus renewable power and storage logic.

Phase 3 — County scale

Use the pilot as a policy and investor reference for wider rollout.

Phase 4 — National template

Position the model as a repeatable Irish energy transition pathway.
